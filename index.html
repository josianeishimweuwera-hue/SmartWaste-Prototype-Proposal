SmartWaste

The Smart Waste Rwanda Prototype is an integrated hardware and software solution designed to modernize solid waste collection and segregation in urban environments like Kigali. By combining IoT-enabled waste bins with automated collection route optimization, this prototype facilitates Rwanda's transition toward a data-driven, circular economy.
